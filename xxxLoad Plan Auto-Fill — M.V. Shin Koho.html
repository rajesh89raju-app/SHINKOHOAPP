Load plan auto-fill

Drop each pour's Trim Calculation PDF in order. Draft, trim and air draft fill in automatically — everything else you type in.

Always cross-check the auto-filled figures against the source PDF and screenshots before using this plan operationally. Amber cells are read from the PDF's text; blue cells (BM%/SF%) are read from a screenshot using OCR, which is less reliable — a red warning line under a row means the screenshot didn't match up cleanly and needs a manual look. This tool does not perform any stability calculation itself.
